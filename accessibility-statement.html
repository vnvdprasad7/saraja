Accessibility Statement

At Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital, we are committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive digital experience that enables all visitors to access healthcare information, book appointments, and use our online services with ease.

We continually work to improve the accessibility and usability of our website by following recognized web accessibility standards and best practices.

01. Our Commitment

We are dedicated to making our website accessible to individuals of all abilities by providing a user-friendly experience that is easy to navigate, understand, and interact with across different devices and assistive technologies.

Our goal is to ensure that every visitor can conveniently access information about our Ayurvedic treatments, doctors, and healthcare services.

02. Accessibility Features

To improve accessibility, our website is designed with features such as:

  • Clear and consistent navigation
  • Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
  • Readable fonts and appropriate text spacing
  • Sufficient color contrast for improved readability
  • Descriptive headings and page structure
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation where applicable
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Accessible online appointment and contact forms

03. Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our website is designed to work with commonly used assistive technologies, including:

  • Screen readers
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Screen magnifiers
  • Voice recognition software
  • Modern web browsers and accessibility tools

While we aim to support a wide range of technologies, compatibility may vary depending on the device, browser, or software being used.

04. Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment at Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital.

We regularly review and improve our website to identify and remove accessibility barriers, enhance usability, and align with evolving accessibility standards and best practices.

05. Third-Party Content

Some areas of our website may include third-party services or embedded content, such as payment gateways, maps, videos, or social media integrations.

While we strive to work with accessible third-party providers, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of content or services that are not under our direct control.

06. Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website or require assistance using our online services, we encourage you to contact us. We will make every reasonable effort to provide the information or support you need in an accessible format.

Your feedback also helps us improve the accessibility of our website.

07. Feedback

We welcome suggestions and feedback regarding the accessibility of our website.

If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have recommendations for improvement, please let us know so that we can continue enhancing the experience for all users.

08. Changes to This Accessibility Statement

Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital may update this Accessibility Statement periodically to reflect improvements to our website, changes in technology, or updates to accessibility standards.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
