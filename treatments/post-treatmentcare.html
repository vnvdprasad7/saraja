SUPPORTING A SMOOTH AND COMFORTABLE RECOVERY

Post-treatment Care

Post-treatment care plays an important role in supporting healing and preventing complications. Following recommended care, healthy habits, and timely follow-ups can help maintain long-term anorectal health.

ESSENTIAL CARE FOR A SMOOTH AND SAFE RECOVERY

Why Post-Treatment Care Matters

Recovery does not end when the procedure is completed. The days and weeks that follow are an important part of the healing process.

  • Support Healing

    Follow recommended wound and local-care instructions to support recovery.

  • Maintain Comfort

    Diet, hydration, and healthy bowel habits can help reduce constipation and straining.

  • Manage Discomfort

    Take prescribed medicines as directed and follow your doctor's advice.

  • Recognise Early

    Knowing which symptoms require medical attention can help you seek timely care.

SIMPLE STEPS FOR EFFECTIVE POST-TREATMENT RECOVERY

Your Post-Treatment Care at a Glance

Following a few simple care steps after treatment can support healing, maintain comfort, and help promote a smoother recovery.

  • Follow Your Prescription

    Take medicines exactly as prescribed. Do not change dosage or stop treatment without medical advice.

  • Eat Right

    Choose fibre-rich, balanced meals and drink plenty of fluids to support bowel movements and healing.

  • Stay Hydrated

    Drink adequate water throughout the day unless you have been given specific fluid restrictions.

  • Keep the Area Clean

    Maintain gentle hygiene and follow local-care instructions provided by your doctor.

  • Move Gently

    Light walking may be encouraged. Avoid heavy lifting, strenuous exercise, and prolonged sitting when advised.

  • Attend Follow-Up

    Keep your follow-up appointments so your doctor can assess healing and guide your recovery.

ESSENTIAL DO’S AND DON’TS FOR A SAFE RECOVERY

Do's & Don'ts

Follow these simple do’s and don’ts to support smooth healing, protect the treated area, and reduce the risk of discomfort or complications during recovery.

check_circleDO

  • Follow your prescribed medicines
  • Drink adequate fluids
  • Eat fibre-rich, balanced meals
  • Keep the treated area clean and dry
  • Walk and stay lightly active as advised
  • Attend all follow-up appointments
  • Report concerning symptoms

cancelDON'T

  • Don't self-medicate
  • Don't ignore constipation
  • Don't strain during bowel movements
  • Don't rub the treated area
  • Don't lift heavy weights too soon
  • Don't skip follow-up visits
NUTRITION TO SUPPORT COMFORTABLE RECOVERY

Foods That Support Recovery

A balanced diet can help maintain comfortable bowel movements and provide nutrients needed during recovery.

  • Fibre-Rich Foods

    Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes.

  • Balanced Meals

    Include protein, healthy fats and essential nutrients.

  • Adequate Fluids

    Drink appropriate fluids throughout the day.

  • Healthy Fats

    Use nuts, seeds and healthy oils in moderation.

A STEP-BY-STEP PATH TO COMPLETE RECOVERY

Recovery Timeline

Recovery is a journey. Each step plays an important role in supporting healing, maintaining comfort, and helping you return to your daily routine with confidence.

01

Immediate Care

Receive instructions about medicines, hygiene, diet, activity, and local care.

02

Early Recovery

Focus on rest, bowel habits, hydration, and following your treatment instructions.

03

Healing Phase

Gradually return to normal activities according to your recovery progress.

04

Follow-Up

Attend your review so your doctor can assess healing and address concerns.

05

Long-Term Health

Maintain healthy bowel habits and follow long-term recommendations.

IMPORTANT WARNING SIGNS THAT REQUIRE MEDICAL ATTENTION

Warning Signs – Seek Medical Advice

Contact your healthcare provider promptly if you notice any unusual, worsening, or persistent symptoms during your recovery.

  • check_circleHeavy or persistent bleeding
  • check_circleSevere or increasing pain
  • check_circleFever, chills, or feeling unwell
  • check_circleIncreasing redness, swelling, or discharge
  • check_circleDifficulty passing urine
  • check_circleSevere constipation or vomiting
  • check_circleAny other symptom that worries you
  • check_circlePersistent or worsening difficulty with bowel movements
