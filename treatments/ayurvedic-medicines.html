TRADITIONAL FORMULATIONS FOR HOLISTIC WELLNESS

What Are Ayurvedic Medicines?

Ayurvedic medicines are traditionally prepared formulations that may be used as part of a comprehensive approach to selected anorectal and bowel-related concerns.

Rather than using the same medicine for everyone, treatment may be planned according to your symptoms, bowel habits, clinical findings, and overall health.

SELECTED CONDITIONS FOR AYURVEDIC CARE

Ayurvedic medicines may be considered for selected conditions after appropriate clinical evaluation.

  • Anal Fissure

  • Digestive & Bowel Discomfort

PERSONALISED SUPPORT FOR BETTER BOWEL HEALTH

Types of Ayurvedic Support

Your treatment plan may include one or more supportive approaches depending on your individual requirements.

Internal Medicines

Selected formulations may be prescribed according to your condition and symptoms.

Local Supportive Care

Diet & Lifestyle

Dietary, hydration, and lifestyle changes may support recovery.

Bowel Health Support

Focus on stool consistency and healthier bowel movements.

SIMPLE HABITS FOR BETTER BOWEL HEALTH

Lifestyle Habits That Make a Difference

Small, consistent lifestyle habits can support healthier bowel function, better digestion, and overall anorectal well-being.

  • Stay Hydrated

    Drink adequate water throughout the day according to your individual needs.

  • Eat Fibre-Rich Foods

    Include appropriate fruits, vegetables, and whole grains.

  • Avoid Excessive Straining

    Respond naturally to the urge and avoid prolonged toilet sitting.

  • Regular Physical Activity

    Daily movement can support healthy bowel function.

  • Follow a Routine

    Maintain a regular bowel routine where appropriate.
