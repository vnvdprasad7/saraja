MCDPA Treatment

Understanding the Ayurvedic approach

What Is MCDPA?

MCDPA is a structured treatment approach that combines different aspects of conservative anorectal care rather than focusing only on temporary symptom relief.

After a detailed clinical evaluation, an individualised treatment plan can be prepared according to your symptoms, bowel habits, lifestyle, and clinical findings.

Personalised Treatment

The appropriate combination of treatment approaches depends on the individual's condition, symptoms, severity, and examination findings.

A COMPREHENSIVE APPROACH TO BETTER WELLNESS

Why MCDPA Takes a Comprehensive Approach

Many anorectal problems can be influenced by multiple factors, including constipation, excessive straining, prolonged sitting, dietary habits, and bowel routines.

MCDPA brings these factors together into a structured plan designed to support better bowel habits and overall anorectal health.

  • Addresses Contributing Factors

    Identifies and addresses factors that may affect anorectal health.

  • Improves Bowel Habits

    Supports healthier and more comfortable bowel routines.

  • Supports Natural Recovery

    Encourages the body’s natural healing and recovery process.

  • Better Comfort & Quality of Life

    Helps improve daily comfort and overall quality of life.

FIVE KEY COMPONENTS FOR HOLISTIC CARE

The Five Pillars of MCDPA

Each component may be incorporated according to your individual symptoms, clinical findings, and treatment requirements.

  • Medicines

    Appropriate medicines may be recommended to help manage discomfort, inflammation, and other identified symptoms based on clinical findings.

  • Constac

    Constac-based bowel management may support softer stools, healthier bowel movements, and reduced straining.

  • Diet

    Fibre-rich foods, adequate hydration, and healthier eating habits can support regular bowel movements and digestive health.

  • Physiotherapy

    Selected patients may benefit from physiotherapy to support pelvic-floor function and healthy bowel coordination.

  • Ayurveda

    Appropriate Ayurvedic therapies may be included as part of a personalised plan to support bowel health and recovery.

SUITABLE CONDITIONS FOR CONSERVATIVE CARE

Conditions That May Be Managed

MCDPA may be considered for selected conditions where conservative treatment is clinically appropriate.

  • Early-Stage Piles

    Selected early-stage haemorrhoids may be managed conservatively when clinically appropriate.

  • Constipation

    Treatment focuses on improving bowel regularity, stool consistency, and healthy bowel habits.

  • Selected Anal Fissures

    Conservative management may be considered after proper clinical evaluation.

  • Bowel Habit Issues

    Irregular timing, prolonged sitting, and unhealthy bowel routines may be addressed.

  • Hard Stool Problems

    Dietary and bowel-management measures may help support smoother and more comfortable bowel movements.

  • Straining-Related Symptoms

    Treatment may focus on reducing excessive straining and improving bowel habits.
