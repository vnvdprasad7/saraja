EVERYDAY HABITS FOR HEALTHY BOWEL FUNCTION

Why Diet & Lifestyle Matter

Your bowel health can be influenced by several everyday factors, including what you eat, how much you drink, your activity levels, and your toilet habits.

Diet

The foods you eat can affect stool consistency and bowel regularity.

Hydration

Adequate fluids can support appropriate stool consistency.

Physical Activity

Regular movement supports overall health and bowel function.

Toilet Habits

Avoiding excessive straining and prolonged sitting can support healthier habits.

FIVE ESSENTIAL HABITS FOR BETTER BOWEL HEALTH

The 5 Foundations of Better Bowel Health

Simple, sustainable habits around fibre, hydration, movement, and healthy bowel routines can support regular bowel movements and better long-term bowel health.

  • Fibre

    Include suitable fibre-rich foods as part of a balanced diet.

  • Fluids

    Maintain adequate hydration according to your needs.

  • Movement

    Stay active and avoid prolonged periods of sitting.

  • Bowel Routine

    Respond naturally and avoid unnecessary straining.

  • Consistency

    Build sustainable habits instead of short-term changes.

NUTRITION FOR HEALTHY BOWEL FUNCTION

Foods to Include

Include a variety of these foods as part of a balanced diet.

  • Fresh Fruits

    Choose a variety of fruits as part of a balanced diet.

  • Vegetables

    Include different vegetables throughout the day.

  • Whole Grains

    Oats, whole-wheat products, and other whole grains.

  • Pulses & Legumes

    Beans and lentils can provide useful dietary fibre.

  • Nuts & Seeds

    Suitable portions can provide fibre and healthy fats.

  • Adequate Fluids

    Water and suitable fluids support hydration.

HABITS AND FOODS TO LIMIT FOR BETTER BOWEL HEALTH

Foods & Habits to Be Mindful Of

Certain dietary patterns may contribute to bowel problems in some individuals.

  • Highly Processed Foods

    Frequent consumption may result in lower fibre intake.

  • Low-Fibre Diets

    Consistently low fibre intake may contribute to constipation.

  • Inadequate Fluids

    Insufficient fluid intake may contribute to harder stools.

  • Irregular Eating Patterns

    An inconsistent routine may make bowel habits harder to maintain.

  • Excessive Toilet Sitting

    Unnecessary toilet sitting can encourage prolonged sitting.

Simple daily habits around hydration, balanced meals, movement, and regularity can support healthy bowel function and overall well-being.

Start Your Day

PERSONALISED DIET AND LIFESTYLE FOR BOWEL HEALTH

Diet & Lifestyle for Common Anorectal Concerns

Healthy habits can support bowel management and help address some contributing factors associated with anorectal symptoms.

  • Constipation

    Fibre, fluids, physical activity, and healthy bowel habits can be important parts of constipation management.

  • Anal Fissure

    Maintaining comfortable bowel movements can be an important part of supportive care.

  • Piles / Haemorrhoids

    Avoiding hard stools and excessive straining may help reduce factors that aggravate symptoms.

  • Recurrent Bowel Problems

    Identifying lifestyle factors can help create a more sustainable bowel-health routine.
