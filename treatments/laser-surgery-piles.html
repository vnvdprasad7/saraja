Laser Surgery for Piles

MINIMALLY INVASIVE LASER CARE FOR COMFORTABLE RECOVERY

What Is Laser Surgery for Piles?

Laser surgery for piles refers to minimally invasive procedures that use controlled laser energy as part of the treatment of selected haemorrhoids. Depending on the technique used and the individual’s condition, it may help address enlarged haemorrhoidal tissue and reduce symptoms.

  • check_circleTargets selected haemorrhoidal tissue
  • check_circleUses controlled and focused laser energy
  • check_circleMay reduce enlarged haemorrhoidal tissue
  • check_circleCan help manage symptoms such as bleeding and discomfort
  • check_circleDesigned to minimise unnecessary tissue disruption
  • check_circleMay be suitable for selected grades of haemorrhoids
  • check_circleTreatment is planned according to individual clinical findings
  • check_circleFollow-up care helps monitor healing and recovery
PRECISION LASER TREATMENT FOR TARGETED RELIEF

Conditions That May Be Considered

A laser device delivers controlled energy to the targeted haemorrhoidal tissue, helping treat the affected area with precision while limiting disruption to surrounding tissues.

  • Targets the Haemorrhoidal Tissue

    Controlled laser energy is applied to the selected area.

  • Reduces Enlarged Tissue

    Treatment may help reduce the size of symptomatic tissue.

  • Addresses Selected Symptoms

    May help manage bleeding, swelling, or prolapse in selected patients.

  • Supports Tissue Shrinkage

    Treated tissue may shrink over time as part of healing.

WHEN LASER TREATMENT MAY BE CONSIDERED

Who May Be Considered?

Laser treatment is not suitable for every patient with piles. It may be considered after clinical evaluation for selected cases.

  • check_circle

    Symptomatic haemorrhoids – Persistent symptoms such as bleeding, discomfort, swelling, or irritation may require further treatment when conservative measures are not sufficient.

  • check_circle

    Recurrent bleeding – Patients experiencing repeated bleeding episodes may be evaluated to determine the underlying cause and whether laser treatment is appropriate.

  • check_circle

    Prolapse associated with haemorrhoids – Haemorrhoidal tissue that protrudes during bowel movements may be considered for treatment depending on its severity and clinical assessment.

  • check_circle

    Enlarged internal haemorrhoids – Selected internal haemorrhoids with significant enlargement may benefit from a suitable minimally invasive procedure.

  • check_circle

    Symptoms not adequately controlled by conservative measures – When dietary changes, bowel-habit modifications, medicines, and other supportive measures do not provide sufficient relief, procedural treatment may be considered.

  • check_circle

    Patients requiring a minimally invasive approach – In selected cases, laser-based treatment may be considered as an alternative to more extensive surgical procedures.

  • check_circle

    Cases where treatment goals are clearly identified – The procedure is planned according to the individual’s symptoms, haemorrhoid characteristics, and expected treatment needs.

infoLaser treatment is not suitable for every patient. A clinical evaluation is necessary before the procedure is recommended.
POTENTIAL BENEFITS OF MINIMALLY INVASIVE LASER CARE

Benefits of Laser-Based Treatment

Laser-based treatment may offer several potential advantages for selected patients, including reduced tissue disruption, less post-procedure discomfort, and a quicker return to everyday activities.

  • Minimally Invasive

    A minimally invasive approach for selected cases.

  • Reduced Tissue Trauma

    Some techniques are designed to limit tissue disruption.

  • Potentially Less Discomfort

    Some patients may experience less post-procedure discomfort.

  • Shorter Recovery

    Recovery may be relatively quick for selected procedures.

  • Earlier Return to Routine

    Some patients may resume everyday activities sooner.

  • Day-Care Approach*

    Some procedures may be performed as day-care treatment.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR TREATMENT OPTIONS

Laser Surgery vs Traditional Surgery

Understanding the differences between laser-based and conventional surgery can help you make an informed treatment decision based on your condition, symptoms, and clinical needs.

Aspect Laser-Based Treatment Conventional Surgery
Approach Minimally invasive for selected cases May involve more extensive tissue removal
Tissue Disruption May involve less tissue disruption Greater tissue disruption in some procedures
Post-Procedure Pain May be lower for selected procedures May involve greater post-operative discomfort
Recovery May be relatively quick Can take longer depending on the procedure
Suitability Selected haemorrhoids May be preferred for certain advanced cases
