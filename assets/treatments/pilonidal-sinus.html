About Pilonidal Sinus

What is Pilonidal Sinus?

A pilonidal sinus is a small opening or tract that develops in the skin near the tailbone, usually within the cleft between the buttocks. It can occur when hair and debris become trapped beneath the skin, sometimes leading to irritation or infection.

When infected, it may cause pain, swelling, redness, tenderness, or fluid and pus discharge. Some people may experience recurring symptoms if the sinus does not heal completely.

Early evaluation can help identify the condition and determine an appropriate treatment plan based on the symptoms and individual needs.

KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Common Symptoms of Pilonidal Sinus

Pilonidal sinus can cause discomfort, swelling, discharge, and recurring irritation around the tailbone. Recognising these symptoms early can help with timely evaluation and appropriate treatment.

  • Pain & Tenderness

    Discomfort or soreness around the affected area, especially while sitting.

  • Tailbone Swelling

    A lump or swelling may develop near the tailbone due to inflammation.

  • Pus or Fluid Drainage

    Low fluid intake can contribute to constipation and make stools harder to pass.

  • Redness & Irritation

    The surrounding skin may become red, irritated, or sensitive.

  • Recurring Infection

    Symptoms may return after temporary improvement or previous treatment.

FACTORS TO CONSIDER

What Can Contribute?

Several lifestyle and skin-related factors may contribute to pilonidal sinus formation, irritation, or recurrence.

  • Ingrown Hair

    Hair can get trapped under the skin and lead to sinus formation.

  • Prolonged Sitting

    Hair can get trapped under the skin and lead to sinus formation.

  • Friction & Pressure

    Repeated friction may irritate the skin around the cleft.

  • Excessive Sweating

    Moisture can lead to irritation and bacterial growth.

  • Poor Local Hygiene

    Improper cleaning can increase the risk of infection and recurrence.
