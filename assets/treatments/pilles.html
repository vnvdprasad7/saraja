About Piles & Symptoms

What is Piles (Hemorrhoids)?

Piles are swollen or enlarged blood vessels around the anus and lower rectum. They may develop due to increased pressure during bowel movements and can be associated with constipation, prolonged sitting, straining or diet and lifestyle factors.

While mild symptoms may sometimes go unnoticed, persistent bleeding, pain, swelling or an anal lump should be evaluated by a qualified specialist.

Common Symptoms of Piles

  • Bleeding During Bowel Movements
  • Anal Lump or Swelling
  • Pain or Discomfort
  • Burning Sensation
  • Difficulty Sitting
  • Anal Itching
  • Discomfort While Passing Stool
  • Blood in Stool
