Reception

At Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital, our reception is thoughtfully designed to provide every patient with a warm, welcoming, and stress-free experience. From the moment you enter, our dedicated reception team ensures smooth registration, appointment scheduling, and personalized assistance. We are committed to making your visit comfortable by offering prompt guidance, clear communication, and compassionate support throughout your healthcare journey.

The spacious and patient-friendly reception area features comfortable seating, a clean and soothing environment, and an organized help desk to efficiently address patient queries. Whether you are visiting for a consultation, treatment, follow-up, or diagnostic services, our staff is always available to assist you with care and professionalism.

Operation Theatre

The Operation Theatre at Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital is designed to provide a safe, sterile, and advanced surgical environment for specialized Ayurvedic and minimally invasive procedures. Equipped with modern infrastructure and maintained under strict sterilization protocols, our operation theatre ensures the highest standards of patient safety and infection control.

Our experienced surgeons, anesthetists, and trained nursing staff work together to perform procedures with precision, care, and clinical excellence. The facility is specially equipped for Kshara Sutra Therapy, laser-assisted proctology procedures, and other specialized treatments for piles, fistula, fissure, and pilonidal sinus.

Pharmacy

The Pharmacy at Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital is dedicated to providing patients with genuine, high-quality Ayurvedic medicines and healthcare essentials under one roof. Stocked with trusted formulations from reputed manufacturers, our pharmacy ensures that every prescription is dispensed accurately, safely, and promptly.

Our qualified pharmacy team works closely with doctors to provide the right medications along with clear guidance on dosage, usage, and treatment duration. We are committed to helping patients understand their prescriptions and achieve the best possible outcomes from their treatment.

General Ward

The General Ward at Dr. Saraja's Ayurvedic Hospital is designed to provide patients with a clean, comfortable, and healing environment throughout their stay. Spacious beds, privacy curtains, proper ventilation, and a hygienic atmosphere ensure comfort while maintaining patient dignity and privacy.

Our dedicated team of doctors, nurses, and support staff offers round-the-clock care, closely monitoring each patient's recovery and ensuring timely medications, treatments, and personalized attention. Every patient receives compassionate care tailored to their individual treatment plan.
