ACCURATE ASSESSMENT FOR THE RIGHT CARE

Common Symptoms We Treat

Recognising these symptoms early can help with timely evaluation and appropriate treatment.

  • Bleeding During Bowel Movements

    Fresh blood while passing stool or on wiping.
  • Pain While Passing Stool

    Pain or discomfort during or after bowel movements.
  • Anal Itching

    Persistent itching around the anal area.
  • Anal Lump

    Swelling or a lump around the anus.
  • Pus Discharge

    Pus or fluid discharge from the anal opening.
  • Burning Sensation

    Burning or irritation in and around the anus.
  • Blood in Stool

    Blood mixed with stool or visible in the toilet.
  • Difficulty Sitting

    Discomfort or pain while sitting for long.
stethoscope
When Should You See a Doctor?

Don’t ignore persistent pain, bleeding, swelling, discharge, or discomfort. Early evaluation can help identify the underlying condition and guide appropriate treatment.
