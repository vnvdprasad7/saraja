About

Dr. G. Venkateswarlu

Dr. G. Venkateswarlu, MS (Ay), BAMS, Gold Medalist, is a renowned Senior Ayurvedic Proctologist and Kshara Sutra Specialist with over 20 years of experience in treating anorectal disorders. He specializes in advanced Ayurvedic and laser treatments for piles, fistula, fissure, pilonidal sinus, constipation, and other colorectal conditions. His expertise lies in combining traditional Ayurvedic principles with modern minimally invasive techniques to deliver safe, effective, and long-lasting results.

With a patient-first approach, Dr. Venkateswarlu is committed to providing personalized care tailored to each individual's condition. His focus on accurate diagnosis, compassionate consultation, and holistic treatment has helped thousands of patients achieve faster recovery and improved quality of life. Known for his ethical practice and clinical excellence, he continues to be a trusted choice for comprehensive anorectal care.
